L63: ### Telnet servers
L64:
L65: RBN spots are available via telnet protocol at the following addresses:
L66: telnet.reversebeacon.net port 7000, for CW and RTTY spots
L67:
L68: telnet.reversebeacon.net port 7001, for FT8 spots
L69:
L70: These nodes run stripped down relay servers specifically designed for maximum throughput. They have no filtering features.
L71:
L72: Our intent is that "retail" DX clusters should connect via telnet to one these nodes, and that end-users should connect to the "retail" nodes.
