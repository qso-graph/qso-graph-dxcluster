L24: ## Before you connect
L25:
L26: This guide covers AR-Cluster Server 6.6.247.0 Stable. If your sysop has enabled a listener password, enter it when prompted after your callsign.
L27:
L28: This manual describes AR-Cluster 6.6 commands for logging programs, DX cluster clients and Telnet terminals. Your node’s feeds, saved settings and access policies determine what is available. Use `HELP` for command help and `SHOW VERSION` when reporting a problem to the sysop.
L29: Use your own callsign and a permitted SSID. A node or peer callsign is reserved for its node connection. Coordinate your client profiles to avoid duplicate logins, and wait for any stated retry interval before reconnecting.
